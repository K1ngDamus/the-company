of every agent's identity and ships on every product from every division.**

> **AMENDED 2026-08-24 on Leon's order (rule 17): the palette is now black,
> gray and red.** The mark's geometry is unchanged — only its colors move. The

## The mark

Interlocking **F + P** sharing a single vertical stem, with a **signal-red dot
terminal**. The geometry is untouched by the 2026-08-24 amendment. Primary form
on screen: the reversed monogram on a coal ground. The app tile is the primary
form where the mark needs its own edge — app icons, avatars, favicons.

## Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Coal** | `#0F0F11` | The ground. Page backgrounds, tiles, and text on a light ground |
| **Graphite** | `#16161A` | Alternating band — the second surface |
| **Slate** | `#1A1A1F` | Raised surfaces: cards, form panels |
| **Pitch** | `#0A0A0C` | Deepest ground — footers |
| **Chalk** | `#F4F4F6` | Primary text on dark |
| **Ash** | `#9EA0A8` | Secondary text |
| **Signal** | `#FF4D4F` | The accent — dot terminal, rules, focus rings, primary buttons |

### The one rule that is not a matter of taste

**Signal red is never a fill behind white text.** Measured, `#F4F4F6` on
`#FF4D4F` is **2.97:1** — well under the 4.5:1 AA floor the company holds itself
to (company law 8). Red fills take **Coal** text, which measures **5.86:1**.

Every value above was measured rather than eyeballed:

| Pair | Ratio | Floor | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chalk on Coal | 17.43:1 | 4.5 | ✓ |
| Ash on Coal | 7.34:1 | 4.5 | ✓ |
| Ash on Slate | 6.64:1 | 4.5 | ✓ |
| Signal on Coal | 5.86:1 | 4.5 | ✓ |
| Coal on Signal (buttons) | 5.86:1 | 4.5 | ✓ |
| Chalk on Signal | 2.97:1 | 4.5 | ✗ **never use** |

Signal clears 4.5:1 on every company ground, so — unlike the Clay it replaces —
it may carry small text. It still never carries meaning alone: links are Chalk
with a Signal underline.

## Type

Unchanged. **Playfair Display 600** for the wordmark and headlines, **Jost 400**
for the COLLECTIVE lockup line (0.42em, uppercase) and all UI.

## Lockups, clear space, minimums

Stacked: mark above, "Front Porch" + COLLECTIVE centered below. Horizontal: mark
left, wordmark right, gap = 1/3 mark width. Clear space all sides = 1/4 mark
width. Minimum tile 20px digital / 8mm print; below 32px use the tile, never the
bare monogram.

**Amended:** on a dark ground the horizontal lockup uses the **reversed
monogram, not the tile** — a Coal tile on a Coal page has no edge to read
against. The tile remains correct wherever it has a foreign background behind
it, which is what an app icon always has.

## Don't

Do not recolor the mark outside the palette, add effects or shadows, stretch it,
or rotate it. Do not place the bare Coal monogram on a dark ground (use
reversed). **Do not put white or Chalk text on a Signal fill.** Violations are
accuracy defects — reviewers flag them like any other.

## Amendment record — 2026-08-24

**What changed.** The palette only. Ink `#201C1A` → Coal `#0F0F11`; Clay
`#B45A38` → Signal `#FF4D4F`; Bone `#F4F1EA` → Chalk `#F4F4F6`; Sand `#ECE3D3`
→ Graphite `#16161A`, with Slate and Pitch added as the raised and deepest
grounds a dark-first system needs. The brand is now dark-first: the reversed
mark is the default, not the exception.

**What did not change.** The mark's geometry — the interlocking F and P on a
shared stem with a dot terminal — is identical. Every path in the four SVGs is
byte-for-byte what it was. The type is identical. The lockup proportions,
clear space and minimums are identical.

**Why Signal is `#FF4D4F` and not a deeper red.** On a dark ground a deep red
goes muddy and fails contrast: `#D93036` measures 4.04:1 on Coal, under the
floor. `#FF4D4F` measures 5.86:1 and can therefore carry text as well as
accents. The trade-off is that it is light for print on white (3.27:1) — it
reads as a graphic mark rather than as body copy there, which is what a logo
dot is. If a print-specific deeper red is ever wanted, that is a separate
amendment and should be tested before it is adopted.

**What this obliges.** The company mark ships on every product from every
division (the standing order above), so this amendment reaches beyond the
website:

1. **The Atlanta Experience** — app icon, splash, about screen, launcher icon,
   and the store listing artwork all carry the old palette.
2. **Print** — business cards and QR codes were produced in Ink and Clay.
3. **The raster set** at the desktop home base needs regenerating from the four
   SVGs in this folder.
4. **Agent identity files** that reference the palette by name.

None of that has been touched. It is listed here so the drift is on the record
rather than discovered later.
